01 / Scope and effective date

This policy applies to the planned App Store offline first release of WILDEN Memo (老野记事) for iPhone and iPad. It does not cover the Mac version or earlier mobile versions with local-network sync enabled. This text has a version date of October 16, 2026. This policy was publicly released and took effect on October 16, 2026. We will update this policy if later versions change the product's behavior.

02 / Data collection and processing

The app processes the card titles and text you enter, today's and frequently used notes, history archives, attachment filenames, and related edit information on your device to display, edit, save and reopen your notes. The planned offline release does not currently include account registration, uploads of notes or attachments to a developer server, advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking. This statement is based on a review of the current code; the final release build and real-world behavior still need verification before publication.

If you choose to email the privacy contact address, we will receive the email address and message content you provide. Please avoid including unnecessary private notes or attachments.

03 / Notes and attachments on your device

The app stores today's and frequently used cards, history archives, and related metadata in Documents/LaoyeMemoMobile.json inside its local app container. Existing attachment files are kept in Documents/Attachments in the same container. The app can preview an attachment that exists and can be opened; it shows an unavailable message if a file is missing. This offline release retains viewing of existing attachments. It does not promise a new attachment-import feature.

These files are not encrypted by the app itself. Device locking, operating-system file protection, and your device settings affect actual access and protection. “Stored locally” does not mean the files are inaccessible to everyone in every circumstance.

04 / Network and cross-device sync

The planned offline first release does not offer cross-device sync and does not require an account. The current version has disabled the automatic local-network discovery, connection, reconnection, and note or attachment transmission entry points used for sync. Structures needed for compatibility with older data remain; our current review found no sync started when the app launches.

Verification so far is based on code review and limited observation in a simulated environment. Physical iPhone and iPad behavior, the final release build, and the continued absence of automatic sync traffic still need verification. If you choose to open this policy, the support page, or an email link, your device will access the chosen website or email service. That is not automatic note sync.

05 / Third-party services

We found no third-party component, advertising, in-app analytics, tracking, or developer-operated account service in the current offline version. Apple's App Store and operating system, system backups you enable, and websites or email services you choose to open may handle related information under their own rules. They should not be confused with automatic uploads of note content by the app. Components in the final release build and App Store privacy disclosures still require review before publication.

06 / Retention and deletion

Notes, history, and attachments generally remain in the device's app container until you edit or delete relevant cards, or an operating-system action affects app data. Deleting a today or frequently used card requires a second confirmation. Once confirmed, the app removes the card from the list and saves a deletion record. Deleting a card does not automatically delete attachment files and does not guarantee removal of history, system backups, or existing copies on other devices. Cancelling deletion leaves the card in place.

Before addressing all local data, remaining attachments, or system backups, identify anything you need to retain and check copies on your device and in backups. We do not claim that an in-app deletion is irreversible secure erasure.

07 / System backups

The app does not offer its own cloud-backup service. iCloud or computer backups of your device depend on your settings, the backup method, and Apple's system rules. This policy does not guarantee that a particular backup includes every note and attachment or that any particular backup can be restored. Deleting a card in the app does not automatically delete existing system backups. If recovery matters to you, verify actual backup completion and recoverability.

08 / Children's privacy

This is a general-purpose local note-taking app. The current version has no child-directed accounts, social feature, or targeted advertising. Parents and guardians should help minors manage their device, note content, and system backups. If you believe information concerning a child was sent to us through the contact email, please contact us below.

09 / Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, and delete cards supported by the app and manage system backups through device settings. The current version has no developer account or online data-export portal. To ask about this policy, address an email you sent us, or make a privacy request, use the email below. We will handle requests as applicable. We generally cannot directly read or remotely delete notes kept only on your device.

10 / Changes to this policy

If a later release adds sync, accounts, third-party services, or otherwise changes data handling, we will update this page before the relevant version is used and show a new effective date. The policy and actual behavior for that version will describe its features. This page does not promise features that have not been implemented.
